Changes made to the original (CC BY-SA 4.0 section 3(a)(1)(B)):
  * Each tile is a single 64x64 cell cut out of the "Light" sheet, without scaling.
  * The D-pad tiles that name directions (up, down, left, right, up and down, left and right) have the white and the dim arms
    exchanged, so the pressed directions are the bright ones; Audune draws them dim.
  * The four PlayStation "colored" face tiles (audune_playstation_*_colored.png) have the dark symbol of the Light-sheet tile
    repainted in the symbol color that the "Dark" sheet uses for the same button; the white disc and the alpha are unchanged.
  * At run time the tiles are fitted into the game's own icon cells (cropped to their visible box, scaled to fit, aspect kept).
    That scaling and the compressed copy in runtime/glyph_art.inc (made by tools/make_glyph_styles.py) are mechanical; the tiles
    are not otherwise altered.
  * Reproduce the tiles with tools/make_audune_tiles.py and the original package.

The pack's own notes name three further sources used in its artwork: the AAP-64 palette by Adigun A. Polack
(https://lospec.com/palette-list/aap-64), the Work Sans font (SIL Open Font License,
https://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site_id=nrsi&id=OFL) and some icons of the Font Awesome Free icon set
(https://fontawesome.com/license/free).

The Kenney Input Prompts tiles used for the keyboard and Steam styles are CC0 and are in ../styles/.
